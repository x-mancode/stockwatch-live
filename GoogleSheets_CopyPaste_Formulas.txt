========================================================================================
GOOGLE SHEETS SMART AUTO-FETCH FORMULAS (TYPE SYMBOL & ENTER)
========================================================================================

Row 1 (Headers):
A1: F&O Stock Symbol
B1: Live Price (₹)
C1: 1D Change (%)
D1: Futures Signal
E1: Trade Action
F1: Stop-Loss Level (₹)
G1: Stop-Loss Risk (%)
H1: Target 1 (1:1 ₹)
I1: Target 2 (1:2 ₹)
J1: Daily Range (ATR)
K1: 50-Day Moving Avg (₹)
L1: 200-Day Moving Avg (₹)
M1: Technical Bias / Setup

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMULAS FOR ROW 2 (Pre-configured for Rows 2 to 100):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Column A: (Type any symbol, e.g. PETRONET, RELIANCE, ZOMATO)

Column B (Live Price with "Not Found" Warning):
=IF(ISBLANK(A2), "", IFERROR(GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:" & A2, "price"), "⚠️ NOT FOUND"))

Column C (1D Change %):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:" & A2, "changepct")/100, 0))

Column D (Futures Signal with Invalid Stock Warning):
=IF(ISBLANK(A2), "", IF(B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND", "⚠️ INVALID STOCK ON NSE", IFERROR(IF(B2="Loading...", "⏳ LOADING", IF(AND(B2>K2, K2>L2), "🟢 STRONG LONG", IF(B2>K2, "🟢 LONG", IF(AND(B2<K2, K2<L2), "🔴 STRONG SHORT", IF(B2<K2, "🔴 SHORT", "⚪ NO TRADE"))))), "⚪ NO TRADE")))

Column E (Trade Action):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("STRONG LONG", D2)), "🟢 BUY FUTURE (High Conviction)", IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("LONG", D2)), "🟢 BUY FUTURE (Pullback Entry)", IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("STRONG SHORT", D2)), "🔴 SELL / SHORT FUTURE (High Conviction)", IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("SHORT", D2)), "🔴 SELL / SHORT FUTURE (Resistance)", "⚪ STAND ASIDE (Wait)")))))

Column F (Stop-Loss Level ₹ - Above for Short, Below for Long):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("LONG", D2)), ROUND(B2 - (1.5 * J2), 2), IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("SHORT", D2)), ROUND(B2 + (1.5 * J2), 2), "-")), "-"))

Column G (Stop-Loss Risk %):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND", NOT(ISNUMBER(F2))), "", IFERROR(ROUND(ABS(B2 - F2) / B2, 4), "-"))

Column H (Target 1 - 1:1 Risk/Reward ₹):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("LONG", D2)), ROUND(B2 + (1.5 * J2), 2), IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("SHORT", D2)), ROUND(B2 - (1.5 * J2), 2), "-")), "-"))

Column I (Target 2 - 1:2 Risk/Reward ₹):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("LONG", D2)), ROUND(B2 + (3.0 * J2), 2), IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("SHORT", D2)), ROUND(B2 - (3.0 * J2), 2), "-")), "-"))

Column J (Daily ATR Volatility Proxy):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(ROUND(MAX(GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:" & A2, "high") - GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:" & A2, "low"), B2 * 0.015), 2), 5))

Column K (50-Day Moving Average):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(ROUND(AVERAGE(INDEX(GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:" & A2, "price", TODAY()-75, TODAY()), , 2)), 2), B2))

Column L (200-Day Moving Average):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IFERROR(ROUND(AVERAGE(INDEX(GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:" & A2, "price", TODAY()-300, TODAY()), , 2)), 2), B2))

Column M (Technical Bias):
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(A2), B2="⚠️ NOT FOUND"), "", IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("LONG", D2)), "🚀 Bullish (Trade with Trend)", IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("SHORT", D2)), "🔻 Bearish Breakdown (Short on Rise)", "↔️ Choppy / Range (Wait for Breakout)")))

========================================================================================
FEATURES:
1. Blank rows stay 100% clean and empty.
2. The moment you type any symbol into Column A and hit Enter, all columns auto-fetch!
3. If a typo or invalid ticker is entered, Column B shows "⚠️ NOT FOUND" and Column D shows "⚠️ INVALID STOCK ON NSE".
