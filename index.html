📊 How Every Indicator & Signal is Calculated
1. Market Regime (Trending vs. Range-Bound)

Uses Kaufman's Efficiency Ratio (ER 14) to detect whether a stock is in a trending wave or sideways consolidation:

Efficiency Ratio = |Net Price Change over 14 Days| / Sum of Absolute Daily Swings

• TRENDING (ER ≥ 0.38): Clean directional momentum. System hunts trend-continuation pullbacks.
• RANGE-BOUND (ER < 0.38): Choppy equilibrium. System hunts mean-reversion bounces off support/resistance.

2. Quantitative Score (-100 to +100)

Blends 4 institutional indicators:
• Trend Alignment (40 pts): Direction vs 20 EMA and 200 EMA (Bullish if above, Bearish if below).
• Momentum (35 pts): RSI(14) expansion above/below 50 line.
• Smart Money Flow (20 pts): Chaikin Money Flow volume accumulation vs distribution.
• Mean Distance Z-Score (25 pts): Normalized distance to 20 EMA in terms of ATR (rewards value pullbacks).

3. Futures Action & Conviction Levels
🟢 BUY FUTURE (Longs)

• Score ≥ +45: High Conviction (Breakout expansion)
• Score +20 to +44: Pullback Entry (Buying value dip at 20 EMA)

🔴 SELL / SHORT FUTURE (Shorts)

• Score ≤ -45: High Conviction Short (Heavy institutional breakdown)
• Score -20 to -44: Resistance Entry (Fading upper band)

• ⚪ STAND ASIDE (Score -19 to +19): Market is in fair-value consolidation. Protects capital from whipsaws.

4. Dynamic Volatility-Based Stop-Loss & Targets (ATR)

Uses 14-period Average True Range (ATR) — measuring actual daily market volatility in Rupees:

For BUY Trades (Long):
• Stop-Loss = Live Price - (1.5 × ATR)
• Target 1 = Live Price + (1.5 × ATR) (1:1 R:R)
• Target 2 = Live Price + (3.0 × ATR) (1:2 High-Reward Runner)

For SHORT Trades (Selling Futures):
• Stop-Loss = Live Price + (1.5 × ATR)
• Target 1 = Live Price - (1.5 × ATR) (1:1 R:R)
• Target 2 = Live Price - (3.0 × ATR) (1:2 Breakdown Target)
